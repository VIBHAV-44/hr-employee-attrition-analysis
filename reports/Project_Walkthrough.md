# Interview Walkthrough
- **Problem:** HR wanted a clear view of workforce attrition patterns.
- **Data:** Synthetic employee data with department, role, tenure, satisfaction, income, overtime and attrition.
- **Preparation:** Checked unique IDs, valid attrition labels and non-negative income.
- **Analysis:** Calculated attrition rate and compared groups using SQL and Pandas.
- **Visualization:** Power BI overview and descriptive-analysis pages.
- **Limitations:** Fictional data; group differences do not prove why employees left.
